PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: July 2026

HYPER X respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information We Collect

  • Account data: username, email, and password (stored securely)
  • Order data: purchase history, payment references, and delivery records
  • Technical data: browser type, IP address, and device info for security
  • Communication: messages sent via WhatsApp, Telegram, or Discord support

How We Use Your Information

  • Process orders and deliver digital products
  • Manage your account and wallet balance
  • Provide customer support
  • Prevent fraud and unauthorized access
  • Improve our website and services

Data Storage

Account and order data is stored locally in your browser (localStorage) and on our systems. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Cookies & Local Storage

We use browser local storage to keep you logged in, save cart data, and remember preferences. You can clear this data anytime from your browser settings.

Third-Party Services

We use third-party payment processors and communication platforms (WhatsApp, Telegram, Discord). These services have their own privacy policies.

Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your data. However, no online service is 100% secure. Use a strong password and do not share your account credentials.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account data by contacting us on our Contact page.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy periodically. Check this page for the latest version.
